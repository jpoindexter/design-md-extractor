Shared root cause: per-viewport scaled values, matched by exact string

The pipeline records every value at desktop/tablet/mobile and counts by exact string. So one 80px radius becomes three tokens (80px, 65.067px, 67.16px); spacing becomes 4.88002px 19.5201px. A single value-normalization layer (round px · prefer the desktop value · cluster near-equal · normalize units) fixes gaps 1, 6 & 7 at once and de-noises 4 & 5.
